Ethics in AI systems starts at the architecture level

Ethical limits belong in the structure of a system, not in a checklist before launch.

Imran Sardar 10 October 2026 5 min read

In many projects ethics arrives last, as one more line in a pre-launch checklist. By then changes are expensive and time is short, so the problems often stay.

Decide in the architecture

Where the data comes from, who can see it, and which decisions stay with a person are three questions best answered on the first day of design.

Ethics is not a feature. It is the foundation of the system.

Three first steps

  • List the user data you truly need.
  • Name one accountable person for every automated decision.
  • Plan a way to roll back before you need it.

Closing thought

The habit pays off even on a small project. A little more thinking at the start saves a lot of trouble later. For more, see the other posts.
